01 / ORIGIN

I'm Nicole, a mechanical engineering student at MIT graduating in 2027.

I grew up in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, where hands-on projects were always part of life. From helping my dad build a backyard sunhouse to fixing my mom's sewing machine, I learned early that the most satisfying problems are the ones you can take apart, understand, and make better.

02 / WHAT I CARE ABOUT

I want to build technology that makes the real world more sustainable.

I'm especially interested in clean energy, water, robotics, and physical systems that improve everyday life. I enjoy working across the full engineering process—from a rough idea and CAD model to fabrication, testing, debugging, and iteration.
